ArtistFolio — Support

Portfolio and enquiry tool for independent artists, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Getting Started

Create your profile

On first launch the app is empty by design. Long-press the app title on the Home tab and authenticate with your device passcode or Face ID to reveal the Dashboard. Then open Artist profile → "Create artist profile" and add your name, bio, contact email, phone number and links.

Add artworks

From the Dashboard or by long-pressing a card in Gallery, add each piece with title, medium, dimensions, year, story and photos. Set status (available, reserved, sold) and an optional private guide price to help judge offers.

QR codes

Dashboard → QR codes creates print-ready codes for wall labels, business cards and table cards. Codes can open an email, a text message, a WhatsApp chat to your number, your contact card, or any web link. Text and WhatsApp codes need your phone number (with country code) set in Artist profile first.

Enquiries and offers

Visitor enquiries, offers and commission requests appear in the Dashboard inbox with a status workflow: new, replied, in progress, sold, closed. When a visitor leaves a phone number you can call, text or open a WhatsApp chat directly from the enquiry.

Subscribers and announcements

Sign-ups build your private subscriber list (CSV export included). Announcements send an update to active subscribers from your own Mail app, or copy the text for a WhatsApp broadcast.

Data & Privacy

Where your data lives

Everything is stored on your device and syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account. Nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for details.
