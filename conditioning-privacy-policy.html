Privacy Policy

Conditioning Coach Analysis

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Overview

Conditioning Coach Analysis is a circuit-training and conditioning app for iPhone and Apple Watch. Your privacy is important. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Workout data: Sessions, circuits, stations, rounds, reps, work/rest times, and estimated calories are stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Health data: With your permission, the app can write conditioning workouts to Apple Health. We do not read your Health data without explicit consent.
  • Profile data: Optional information such as name, age, body weight, height, sport, and training preferences is stored locally on your device.
  • Subscription status: StoreKit purchase/entitlement status is handled by Apple and verified on-device.

How Data is Used

  • To provide in-app analytics, trends, and performance summaries.
  • To generate training recommendations and session prescriptions.
  • To sync conditioning workouts to Apple Health if you grant access.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. All personal and workout data remains on your device except when you choose to share it (e.g., HealthKit, AirDrop, or the system Share sheet).

Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app subscriptions and Apple's HealthKit for optional workout sync. These services are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • You can delete the app and its locally stored data at any time from iOS Settings.
  • You can manage or revoke Apple Health permissions from the iOS Health app.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your Apple ID account settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit the Support page.
