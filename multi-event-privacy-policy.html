Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data Collection

Multi Event Coach Analysis collects workout data that you manually enter, including decathlon and heptathlon event results, training sessions, and competition sessions. GPS coordinates may be stored with training sessions for map display. No HealthKit data, camera, microphone, or other device sensor data is accessed.

Data Storage

Your workout data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. User profile data is stored in UserDefaults. No workout data is sent to external servers or cloud services. All data remains private on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties. All your workout data remains private on your device. The Share Performance feature generates an image or file that you choose to share — no data is transmitted automatically.

Location Data

Multi Event Coach Analysis may store GPS coordinates with your training sessions for map display in session detail. Location data is stored on-device only and is not transmitted to external servers.

No HealthKit or Sensor Access

Multi Event Coach Analysis does not access HealthKit, camera, microphone, accelerometer, or any other device sensors. All event results and workout data are manually entered by you.

Notifications

If you enable workout reminders, Multi Event Coach Analysis uses local notifications on your device to remind you to log your sessions. These notifications are processed entirely on-device and are not sent from any server.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings > Reset All Data. This permanently removes all workouts, profile information, and settings from your device.

Subscription and Trial

Multi Event Coach Analysis Pro offers a 1-week free trial, then a subscription. If you do not subscribe after the trial, you will continue to have access to free features including manual event entry and workout history. Pro features such as advanced analytics, event comparisons, and performance sharing will be locked. Your workout data is never deleted when the trial expires. Subscribing to Multi Event Coach Analysis Pro gives you access to advanced features but does not change our data collection or privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Multi Event Coach Analysis is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children. The app does not collect personal data from any user — all data is self-entered workout information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@accel-8.com.
