Getting Started

1. Wear your Apple Watch overnight to capture HRV data.

2. Authorize HealthKit access when prompted on first launch.

3. Your first Readiness Score will appear after 7 days of data (baseline establishment period).

4. Optionally, complete the 2-tap daily check-in (soreness + motivation) to enrich your score.

No Reading Yet?

If you see "No Reading Yet," ensure:

  • Your Apple Watch is worn overnight to capture HRV.
  • HealthKit access is authorized (Settings > Privacy > Health > Recovery Coach Analysis).
  • You haven't reached the 7-day baseline threshold yet.

Subscription

Recovery Coach Pro is £1.99/month with a 1-week free trial. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings at any time.

Linked Viewer (Coach/Parent)

To share your readiness data with a coach or parent, go to More > Linked Devices > Invite. The linked viewer gets read-only access and you can revoke it at any time.

Not a Medical Device

Recovery Coach Analysis is a training recovery tool, not a medical device. The Readiness Score does not diagnose overtraining, burnout, or any medical condition. Always consult a qualified professional for health concerns.
