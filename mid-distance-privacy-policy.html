Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data Collection

Mid Distance Analysis collects workout data that you manually enter, including running session times, distances, splits, drill entries, stride data, and training sessions. The app optionally requests location access to fetch weather conditions (temperature, wind speed, humidity) for your training sessions. If granted, your GPS coordinates are stored with the workout session and sent to a weather API service to retrieve conditions. Location is only accessed when you create a new session and can be skipped by entering a location name manually. No HealthKit data, camera, microphone, or other device sensor data is accessed.

Data Storage

Your workout data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. User profile data is stored in UserDefaults. No workout data is sent to external servers or cloud services. When location-based weather fetch is used, your GPS coordinates are stored on-device with the workout session for map display in session detail, and are sent to the Open-Meteo weather API to retrieve conditions — this data is used only for the weather request and is not shared with any other party.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties. All your workout data remains private on your device. The Share Performance feature generates an image or file that you choose to share — no data is transmitted automatically.

Location Access

Mid Distance Analysis may request location access (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription) to automatically fetch weather conditions for your training sessions. Location access is optional — you can decline and enter a location name manually. When used, your GPS coordinates are stored on your device with the workout session for map display in session detail, and are sent to the Open-Meteo weather API to retrieve temperature, wind, and humidity data. Your location is not shared with any other third party and is never transmitted except for the one-time weather request.

No HealthKit or Sensor Access

Mid Distance Analysis does not access HealthKit, camera, microphone, accelerometer, or any other device sensors. All running times and workout data are manually entered by you.

Notifications

If you enable workout reminders, Mid Distance Analysis uses local notifications on your device to remind you to log your sessions. These notifications are processed entirely on-device and are not sent from any server.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings > Reset All Data. This permanently removes all workouts, profile information, and settings from your device.

Subscription and Trial

Mid Distance Analysis Pro offers a 1-week free trial, then monthly and annual subscription options. If you do not subscribe after the trial, you will continue to have access to free features including manual workout entry, run analysis, and workout history. Pro features such as advanced charts, trends, and export will be locked. Your workout data is never deleted when the trial expires. Subscribing to Mid Distance Analysis Pro gives you access to advanced features but does not change our data collection or privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Mid Distance Analysis is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children. The app does not collect personal data from any user — all data is self-entered workout information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy at any time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@accel-8.com.
