Terms of Use & Content Guidelines

Last updated: October 2026

ArtistFolio is a portfolio and enquiry tool for independent artists. Artists publish a public gallery of their own work; visitors can browse published galleries, send enquiries and offers, and subscribe to updates.

Publishing Your Gallery

By publishing a gallery you confirm that:

  • You are the creator of the artwork shown, or you have the right to display and offer it.
  • The information in your profile and listings is accurate.
  • You will respond to enquiries and offers in good faith. Any sale is agreed directly between you and the buyer — the app does not process payments.

Content Guidelines

Published galleries must not contain:

  • Illegal content, hate speech, harassment, or incitement to violence.
  • Sexually explicit material. Fine-art nudity is permitted where lawful.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademarks, or other rights — including images of artwork you did not create and are not authorised to sell.
  • Misleading listings, scams, or impersonation of another artist or person.
  • Personal data of third parties published without their consent.

Reporting & Moderation

Every published gallery and artwork has a "Report" option. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Where a report is upheld we unpublish the gallery, and repeat or serious violations lead to removal. To appeal a moderation decision, contact us at the address below.

Enquiries, Offers & Sales

The app passes enquiries and offers between visitors and artists. It is not a party to any sale: payment, shipping, returns and disputes are matters for the artist and buyer directly. Prices shown are set by the artist.

Availability

The app and the hosted artist directory are provided as-is. We may suspend or remove a gallery that breaches these terms or that we are required to remove by law.

Contact

Questions, reports and appeals: support@accel-8.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
