Frequently Asked Questions

How do I build a custom circuit?

Open the app, tap New Session, then add stations, set work/rest intervals, rounds, and save it to your library.

Can I use the app without an Apple Watch?

Yes. All circuit coaching works on iPhone. The Apple Watch app adds wrist-based live coaching and progress tracking.

What does Conditioning Coach Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks advanced analytics, trends, shareable reports, and full access to the history and trends screens. The first week is free, then a monthly or annual subscription applies.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions and select Conditioning Coach Analysis. Cancelling stops auto-renewal; you keep access until the current period ends.

Why isn't my Apple Watch showing the session?

Make sure the watch app is installed, your iPhone and Apple Watch are connected, and Bluetooth/Wi-Fi are enabled. Start a session on iPhone and tap the watch option, or pick a quick-start session from the watch Home view.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. Workout and profile data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If iCloud sync is enabled in a future update, it will be clearly communicated and optional.
