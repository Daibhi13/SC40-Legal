Getting Started

Welcome to Strength Coach Analysis

Strength Coach Analysis learns your strength curve, tracks your recovery, and tells you exactly what to lift next. Log exercises, sets, reps, and weights, get AI-powered load prescriptions, and generate shareable performance reports.

Quick Start Guide

  • 1. Complete the onboarding flow — answer questions about your experience, goals, schedule, and equipment
  • 2. Open the Home tab to see your AI daily recommendation
  • 3. Tap "Start This Session" — every exercise is pre-filled with the right weight, reps, sets, and RPE
  • 4. After your workout, review your AI feedback and check analytics in the Trends tab

What You'll Need

  • An iPhone running iOS 17.6 or later
  • Apple Watch (optional, for live workout tracking)
  • Access to your gym or training space
  • 30-90 minutes per session

Training Frequency

The app recommends 2-6 sessions per week based on your training profile. Recovery and progressive overload are key.

Workouts

Starting a Session

Use the Home tab to start an AI-recommended session, or browse the Session Library for 46 predefined templates. You can also build a custom workout from scratch in the New Session view.

Exercise Library

Browse 75 built-in exercises across all major muscle groups with step-by-step instructions, equipment requirements, and difficulty ratings. Add your own custom exercises too. Filter by equipment level (full gym, home gym, minimal).

Session Templates

Choose from 46 predefined session templates across 9 categories: Full Body, Upper/Lower, Push/Pull/Legs, Body Part Split, Power, Functional Strength, Abs & Core, Endurance, and Peaking.

Adaptive Load Prescription

The app predicts your one-rep max using on-device machine learning, then prescribes exact weight, reps, sets, and rest for each exercise. As you get stronger, the weights automatically increase. When you're fatigued, they back off.

Apple Watch Live Tracking

Track workouts directly on your Apple Watch. Log sets, reps, weight, and RPE from your wrist. Rest timer with wrist haptics keeps you on schedule. Completed sessions sync back to your phone automatically.

Troubleshooting

Workout Not Saving

  • Make sure you've added at least one exercise with a set before saving
  • Check that the session has a name and date
  • Try force quitting and reopening the app

Per-Exercise Chart Shows No Data

  • Save at least two workouts containing the same exercise
  • Check the time range filter in Analytics
  • Ensure the exercise name is consistent across sessions

Prescriptions Show "No Data Yet"

  • Log at least one session with the exercise to generate a baseline
  • Complete a guided 1RM test for the exercise to get accurate prescriptions faster
  • The ML predictor needs 12+ sets per exercise to become fully personalised

Apple Health Not Syncing

  • Enable Apple Health permission in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices
  • Ensure "Strength Coach Analysis" is allowed to write workouts and body mass
  • Body mass, sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate must be present in Health for recovery scoring

Apple Watch Not Receiving Sessions

  • Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on your iPhone
  • Open the Watch app on your iPhone to verify the companion app is installed
  • Restart both devices if the connection doesn't establish

Subscription or Trial Issues

  • Restore purchases in More > Restore Purchases
  • For testing, run in the iOS Simulator with the Xcode StoreKit Configuration selected
  • Contact support if the subscription status does not update

App Crashes or Freezes

  • Force quit and reopen the app
  • Restart your device
  • Contact support at support@accel-8.com if issues persist

FAQ

General

What is Strength Coach Analysis?

An AI-powered strength training coach for iOS and Apple Watch that logs exercises, predicts your 1RM with on-device machine learning, prescribes exact loads, tracks recovery from Apple Health, and generates shareable performance reports.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The app works entirely on iPhone. Apple Watch support is included for live workout tracking from your wrist, including set logging, rest timer, and RPE entry.

Is Strength Coach Analysis suitable for beginners?

Yes. Beginner mode automatically activates for new lifters, providing simplified feedback, educational tooltips, a visual RPE scale, and a "What do these mean?" guide. Advanced features like mesocycle planning are hidden until you're ready.

Training & Prescription

How does the app know what weight to prescribe?

The app predicts your one-rep max using on-device machine learning (linear regression with feature normalization). Until you've logged 12+ sets per exercise, it falls back to a weight-dependent formula calibrated on 303,000+ near-failure sets. Guided 1RM tests are weighted 10x as ground-truth anchors.

What is a mesocycle?

A mesocycle is a 4-6 week training block with built-in progressive overload and deload weeks. The app auto-generates one during onboarding and offers linear, undulating, or block periodization schemes.

What is the recovery score?

A 0-100 score computed from your Apple Health data: sleep duration (last 3 nights), HRV trend (last 7 days), resting heart rate trend (last 7 days), and accumulated training load. High recovery increases prescribed intensity; low recovery dials it back.

Can I log a 1RM test?

Yes. Tap the 1RM test button on any exercise to log a guided max-effort test. These are used as ground-truth anchors for the ML predictor, so your prescriptions become more accurate over time.

Health & Subscriptions

What HealthKit data is used?

With your permission, the app writes strength workouts and body weight to Apple Health, and reads body mass, biological sex, date of birth, sleep duration, HRV, and resting heart rate for recovery scoring and profile personalisation.

What are Pro features?

Pro includes adaptive load prescription, mesocycle planning, per-exercise trend analysis, full analytics, shareable reports (PDF/CSV/text), plateau detection, and Apple Watch Pro features (ML prescriptions, RPE entry, AI feedback). Pro is £4.99/month or £49.99/year, both with a 1-week free trial. A lifetime option is available for £149.99.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to More > Restore Purchases and sign in with your Apple ID to restore an active subscription or lifetime purchase.

Technical

Is my data backed up?

Workout data is stored locally on your device with SwiftData. iCloud sync is available when enabled. Use the export/share features to back up important data.

Can I export my workout data?

Yes, Pro users can export workout history as CSV or PDF and share professional reports.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Core workout entry, history, analytics, and load prescription work without an internet connection. Subscriptions and Apple Health sync require a connection.

Does the app support iPad?

Yes. The app supports portrait and landscape on iPhone and all four orientations on iPad, with adaptive layouts for larger screens.
