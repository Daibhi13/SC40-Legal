Sprint Coach Analysis

Analyze your sprint performance, track your progress, and get instant insights to improve your speed on the track.

Features

  • Manual Sprint Timing — Log reps for any distance (30m, 60m, 100m, 200m, 400m) with run types including Standing Start, Blocks, Flying, and Tempo.
  • Performance Analysis — Speed and acceleration scores normalized to elite-level benchmarks. Your sprint profile (Elite, Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner) is based on your 100m-equivalent pace.
  • Personal Best Tracking — PBs automatically detected for 60m, 100m, 200m, and 400m. Manually enter or edit in Edit Profile.
  • Drills & Strides — Log drills (A Skips, Bounds, Wall Drives) and stride distances (50m–200m) for each session.
  • Training vs Race Sessions — Tag sessions as Training or Race. Race sessions get dedicated analysis in the Race tab.
  • Weather Conditions — Automatic temperature, humidity, and wind data based on session location.
  • Per-Distance Trend Analysis — Track your times over time for each distance Pro
  • Split Timing Breakdowns — 200m & 400m split analysis Pro
  • Coach Feedback — AI-powered insights on your performance Pro
  • Performance Reports — Share reports with your team or coach Pro
