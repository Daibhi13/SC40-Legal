Throws Coach Analysis — Support

Throwing Events Performance Analysis for iOS.

Getting Started

Logging a Session

Tap 'New Session' to log a throw workout. Select your throwing event, enter distances for each attempt, and save when done.

Throwing Events

Supported Events

Throws Coach Analysis supports shot put, discus, javelin, and hammer throw.

Wind Readings

Wind readings can be recorded with each throw session. Wind conditions are tracked alongside performance to help you understand how conditions affect your distances.

Understanding Trends

The Trends tab shows per-event analysis including best distances, average distances, and trend direction. Select a specific event or view all events at once.

Subscription

Throws Coach Analysis Pro

Pro unlocks advanced analytics, performance sharing, and more. Enjoy a 1-week free trial when you first install the app.

Data & Privacy

Local Storage

All session data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to a server. Optional location access may be used to fetch weather conditions for your sessions.
