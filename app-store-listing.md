## App Description (4000 chars max)

Sprint Coach 40 is your professional real-time sprint performance tracking app for the 40-yard dash. Using your Apple Watch and iPhone together, it captures precise GPS sprint timing, acceleration data, and biomechanical metrics during your training sessions.

Built for football combine prep, track athletes, and sprint coaches who need accurate, automated timing without hand timers.

FEATURES

• Real-Time GPS Sprint Timing
Your Apple Watch uses GPS to accurately measure sprint distances and speeds during outdoor workouts. Location data is accessed during active workouts, including when the screen is off or the app is in the background.

• Apple Watch Integration
Full Apple Watch companion app with live workout tracking. Start sprints from your wrist, see real-time metrics, and sync completed sessions back to your iPhone automatically.

• Acceleration & Biomechanics
Motion sensors detect sprint starts, measure acceleration, and provide biomechanical analysis including arm swing and stride detection.

• Heart Rate Tracking
With your permission, Sprint Coach 40 reads heart rate data from HealthKit during workouts and saves completed sessions to Apple Health.

• Weather Conditions
Automatically fetches weather conditions (temperature, wind, humidity) for your training sessions using your location.

• Performance Analysis
Speed scores, acceleration scores, and sprint profiles (Elite, Advanced, Intermediate, or Beginner) based on your times and distances.

• Workout History
Browse all past sessions with full detail — every sprint, split, drill, and analysis result.

• Share Performance (Pro)
Generate professional reports with personal bests, statistics, and session history. Share via email, message, or PDF with coaches and recruiters.

• Workout Reminders
Set custom reminder notifications for your training days so you never miss a session.

## App Review Information

### Review Notes
Sprint Coach 40 is a real-time GPS sprint timing app that uses an Apple Watch companion for workout tracking. The app uses the following device capabilities:
- CoreLocation (GPS) for measuring sprint distances and speeds during active workouts, including background location for continuous tracking
- HealthKit to read heart rate during workouts and save workout sessions to Apple Health
- Motion sensors (accelerometer and DeviceMotion) for sprint start detection and biomechanical analysis
- Barometer/altimeter for elevation validation on hills/slopes
- Bluetooth for Apple Watch communication and external heart rate monitors
- Local notifications for workout reminders and rest timers

All sensor data is processed on-device. No data is sent to external servers except GPS coordinates sent to the Open-Meteo weather API for weather conditions. There is no login or account system.

### Demo Instructions
1. Launch the app on iPhone and complete onboarding (name, age, sport, position, personal best).
2. Start a sprint workout from the Apple Watch app.
3. Run a sprint — GPS will track distance and time automatically.
4. After the sprint, check the iPhone app for detailed analysis (speed score, acceleration, biomechanics).
5. Go to History to view saved sessions.
6. Go to More tab to explore Share Performance, Settings, and Help.
7. In Settings, verify Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links are accessible.
