Plyometrics Coach Analysis — Support

Plyometric Training Performance Analysis for iOS.

Getting Started

Logging a Session

Tap 'New Session' to create a plyometric workout. Choose your exercises, then log sets with reps, contacts, load, and RPE for each. Save when done.

Exercises

Supported Exercises

Plyometrics Coach Analysis supports box jumps, depth jumps, bounds, medicine ball throws, and more. You can also log custom exercises with manual entry.

Ground Contact Times

Ground contact times can be recorded for each rep to track reactive strength and elasticity. Shorter contact times generally indicate better reactive ability.

Volume & Power Tracking

The app tracks total training volume (contacts and load) and power output across sessions, helping you monitor training load and progression over time.

Apple Health Integration

HealthKit

Plyometrics Coach Analysis can optionally save workouts to Apple Health. The app may also read body mass, biological sex, date of birth, sleep analysis, HRV (SDNN), and resting heart rate from Apple Health to personalize your analytics and recovery scoring. This integration is optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time in settings.

Subscription

Plyometrics Coach Pro

Pro unlocks advanced analytics, volume trends, power profiling, performance sharing, and more. Enjoy a 1-week free trial when you first install the app.

Data & Privacy

Local Storage

All session data is stored locally on your device. No data is sent to a server. Apple Health data is only accessed with your permission and used solely for personalization within the app.
